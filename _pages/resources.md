- **[ImageJ / FIJI](https://fiji.sc/)**: free, open-source image analysis for measuring, counting and quantifying microscopy images, with a plugin for almost everything.
- **[protocols.io](https://www.protocols.io/)**: a platform for sharing, versioning and discovering lab protocols. Free for publishing public protocols, with paid plans for private and team features.
- **[Cochrane Training](https://training.cochrane.org/)**: learning resources for systematic reviews and meta-analysis. Many are free; some courses require a subscription.
